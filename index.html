View-Dependent Proxy

WHY A PROXY?

Preserving a rare material under real-time constraints.

I wanted the user to feel as though they were holding a genuine opal, one of the world's rarest and most valuable gemstones.

In my previous article, I explored how I rendered the opal's complex, view-dependent appearance. As of summer 2026, however, that rendering process is too computationally demanding to run in real time on current-generation AR glasses.

The solution is to use a proxy. In computer graphics, a proxy is a lightweight representation that stands in for a more expensive asset or process. It preserves the properties required by the experience while reducing the computational cost of rendering and interaction. In this case, the proxy replaces live optical simulation with a set of pre-rendered views selected according to the viewer's position.
